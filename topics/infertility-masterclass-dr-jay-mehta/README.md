# 🎓 Infertility Masterclass by Dr. Jay Mehta

Part of the **[MedEd Open Platform](../../README.md)**.

## 📌 Overview

This comprehensive clinical masterclass series organizes and chronologically restructures the 70+ in-depth video lectures and webinars delivered by **Dr. Jay Mehta** (Shree IVF Clinic, Mumbai).

The curriculum spans over **24+ Hours of structured video training** across **8 core clinical phases**, covering basic endocrine physiology, non-IVF ovulation induction, andrology & micro-TESE, advanced IVF stimulation (Antagonist, PPOS, DuoStim), thin endometrium & regenerative PRP, embryology & vitrification, recurrent implantation failure (RIF) & reproductive immunology, and reproductive surgical techniques.

## ✨ Interactive Platform Features

* **Chronological Navigation:** Sequential playback of lectures organized by clinical phase rather than unsorted upload dates.
* **Synchronized YouTube Player:** Direct embedded streaming with responsive playback on desktop and mobile devices.
* **High-Yield Clinical Summaries:** Evidence-based diagnostic tables, criteria cutoffs, and dosage guidelines rendered alongside videos.
* **Offline Progress Tracking:** Check off completed masterclasses; your progress is automatically saved to browser `localStorage`.
* **Instant Full-Text Search:** Filter across all 42+ structured masterclasses by topic name, hormones, or surgical keywords.
