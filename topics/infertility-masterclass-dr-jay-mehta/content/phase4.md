# Phase 4: Advanced Ovarian Stimulation Protocols in IVF

Mastering IVF protocols: GnRH Antagonist, GnRH Agonist Long, PPOS, Low AMH stimulation, Endometriosis/Adenomyosis IVF strategies, and OHSS prevention.

## 📋 Phase Syllabus & Video Lectures

### Lecture 1: GnRH Antagonist (SHORT) Protocol: Modern Gold Standard
<!-- video:id="_YrikNxrFqo", duration="26:30", mcNumber=10 -->
* **Video ID:** `_YrikNxrFqo` | **Duration:** 26:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #10

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Fixed protocol (start antagonist on Day 6 of stimulation) vs Flexible protocol (start when lead follicle reaches ≥14 mm).
* Agonist trigger (e.g. Leuprolide 0.2 mg / Triptorelin 0.2 mg) completely eliminates early OHSS.
* Shorter duration of stimulation (9–10 days) and lower total gonadotropin dose compared to Long protocol.

### Lecture 2: GnRH Agonist (LONG) Protocol: Indications & Technique
<!-- video:id="i66pq5vD2Oc", duration="22:15", mcNumber=9 -->
* **Video ID:** `i66pq5vD2Oc` | **Duration:** 22:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #9

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Initiated in mid-luteal phase (Day 21) of preceding cycle to achieve complete pituitary desensitization.
* Ensures homogeneous follicular cohort growth; highly useful in normal responders seeking fresh transfer.
* Contraindicated in high responders / PCOS due to high OHSS risk with mandatory hCG trigger.

### Lecture 3: Progesterone Primed Ovarian Stimulation (PPOS) Protocol
<!-- video:id="Q3TSht5X3pM", duration="28:40", mcNumber=35 -->
* **Video ID:** `Q3TSht5X3pM` | **Duration:** 28:40 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #35

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Uses oral medroxyprogesterone acetate (MPA 10 mg/day) or dydrogesterone from Day 2–3 of stimulation to prevent premature LH surge.
* Eliminates expensive injectable GnRH antagonists.
* Mandates a 'Freeze-All' strategy because premature progesterone exposure alters endometrial receptivity.

### Lecture 4: Stimulation Protocols for Low AMH / Poor Responders in IVF

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Poseidon Group 3 & 4 stratification.
* High starting FSH (300 IU) + rLH/hpHMG (150 IU) in a 2:1 ratio.
* DuoStim (Double Stimulation in Follicular + Luteal phases) to accumulate blastocysts within a single menstrual cycle.

### Lecture 5: IVF Stimulation Protocols in Endometriosis & Adenomyosis
<!-- video:id="vYZGAvGQYT8", duration="25:50", mcNumber=75 -->
* **Video ID:** `vYZGAvGQYT8` | **Duration:** 25:50 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #75

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Pre-treatment with ultra-long GnRH agonists (Depot 3.75 mg for 1–3 months) to reduce pelvic inflammatory cytokines.
* High starting dose of gonadotropins to overcome chronic granulosa cell resistance.
* Freeze-all blastocysts; perform frozen embryo transfer after adequate downregulation.

### Lecture 6: Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS): Prevention & Management
<!-- video:id="hAcxDBtEyks", duration="23:20", mcNumber=37 -->
* **Video ID:** `hAcxDBtEyks` | **Duration:** 23:20 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #37

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Primary prevention: GnRH Antagonist protocol + GnRH Agonist trigger (100% OHSS-free pathway).
* Dopamine agonists (Cabergoline 0.5 mg/day for 8 days post-OPU) to block VEGF receptor phosphorylation.
* Mandatory Freeze-All strategy whenever serum E2 >4,000 pg/mL or follicle count >20.
