# Phase 2: Ovulation Induction Protocols & IUI Optimization

Step-by-step stimulation protocols for natural conception and IUI in normal responders, low AMH, PCOS, endometriosis, and unexplained infertility.

* Letrozole 2.5 mg/day (Days 3–7) as first line; lower multiple pregnancy risk compared to CC.

### Lecture 2: Low AMH Ovarian Stimulation Protocols for Natural Conception
<!-- video:id="_8J09tQOAaY", duration="19:05", mcNumber=21 -->
* **Video ID:** `_8J09tQOAaY` | **Duration:** 19:05 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #21

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Avoid high-dose gonadotropin step-up protocols in natural/IUI cycles.
* Mild stimulation: Letrozole 5 mg (Days 2–6) + low dose gonadotropin (75 IU on alternate days).
* Pre-treatment with DHEA 25 mg TID and CoQ10 300 mg/day for 8–12 weeks.

### Lecture 3: PCOS Stimulation in Non-IVF Cycles: Step-Up & Extended Letrozole
<!-- video:id="oiBGXFnPTyg", duration="24:30", mcNumber=13 -->
* **Video ID:** `oiBGXFnPTyg` | **Duration:** 24:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #13

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Step-wise Letrozole titration (2.5 mg → 5 mg → 7.5 mg).
* Extended Letrozole (Days 3–9 or 11) in resistant PCOS.
* Strict cancellation criteria: ≥3 follicles >16 mm to prevent OHSS and high-order multiples.

### Lecture 4: Ovulation Induction in Severe PCOS for IUI Cycles: High Dose Regimens

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Combining extended Letrozole with ultra-low dose step-up FSH (37.5–50 IU/day).
* Frequent serial TVS monitoring to detect sudden explosive multifollicular recruitment.
* Low threshold for cycle cancellation or conversion to IVF.

### Lecture 5: Stimulation Protocols for Thin PCOS in Natural Conception
<!-- video:id="PSTy06fwjJM", duration="18:40", mcNumber=77 -->
* **Video ID:** `PSTy06fwjJM` | **Duration:** 18:40 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #77

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Lean/Thin PCOS phenotypes often exhibit severe LH hypersecretion and extreme follicular sensitivity.
* Low starting dose of Letrozole (2.5 mg) and minimal gonadotropins.
* Careful LH surge monitoring to prevent premature luteinization.

### Lecture 6: Stimulation Protocols for Natural Pregnancy in Endometriosis
<!-- video:id="IcJwIrxAd-g", duration="22:00", mcNumber=76 -->
* **Video ID:** `IcJwIrxAd-g` | **Duration:** 22:00 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #76

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Aromatase inhibitors (Letrozole) reduce local peritoneal and ovarian estrogen production.
* Short duration stimulation to minimize inflammatory flare of endometriotic implants.
* Strict 3–4 cycle limit before advising laparoscopic surgery or IVF.

### Lecture 7: How to Enhance Your IUI Results & Timing Strategies
<!-- video:id="83L0owWShcE", duration="22:15", mcNumber=15 -->
* **Video ID:** `83L0owWShcE` | **Duration:** 22:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #15

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Benchmark target: Post-wash Total Motile Sperm Count (TMSC) > 5–10 Million.
* Optimal endometrial thickness: 8–12 mm with distinct trilaminar architecture.
* IUI vs IVF triage: Transition to IVF after 3–4 failed well-stimulated IUI cycles in women <35y, or 2 cycles in women >35y.

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Essential equipment: Class II Laminar Flow / Biosafety Cabinet, 37°C dry block warmer, centrifuge with soft brake, and phase-contrast microscope.
* Consumables: Non-toxic IVF-grade IUI catheters, silicone-free syringes, and certified density gradient media.
* Strict double-witness identification protocol to eliminate sample mixing errors.
