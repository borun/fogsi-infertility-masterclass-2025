# Phase 3: Male Factor Subfertility & Andrology Workup

Semen analysis interpretation (WHO 6th Edition), sperm preparation techniques, hyperviscosity, teratozoospermia, Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI), micro-TESE, and genetics.

### Lecture 1: How to Interpret Semen Analysis (WHO 6th Edition Criteria)
<!-- video:id="nSwarWgvRuI", duration="25:30", mcNumber=20 -->
* **Video ID:** `nSwarWgvRuI` | **Duration:** 25:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #20

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Standard WHO reference ranges: Volume ≥1.4 mL, Concentration ≥16 M/mL, Total Count ≥39 M, Progressive Motility ≥30%, Normal Morphology ≥4%.
* Total Motile Sperm Count (TMSC = Vol × Conc × Motility / 100) is the definitive clinical triaging metric.
* Repeat testing after 4–6 weeks before establishing chronic male factor diagnoses.

### Lecture 2: Semen Preparation Techniques in IUI: Swim-Up vs Density Gradient
<!-- video:id="qbsUgbb3eVc", duration="19:15", mcNumber=16 -->
* **Video ID:** `qbsUgbb3eVc` | **Duration:** 19:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #16

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Swim-Up: Ideal for normozoospermic samples; lowest reactive oxygen species (ROS) and mechanical damage.
* Double Density Gradient (40%/80%): Best for oligozoospermia, viscous samples, debris, and infectious clearance.
* Avoid excessive centrifugation speeds (>300–400g) which cause sublethal sperm membrane damage.

### Lecture 3: Semen Hyperviscosity & High Liquefaction Time: Clinical Protocol
<!-- video:id="JNZu-bcc0aU", duration="16:45", mcNumber=57 -->
* **Video ID:** `JNZu-bcc0aU` | **Duration:** 16:45 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #57

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Delayed liquefaction (>60 min) or high viscosity indicates accessory sex gland infection (prostatitis/vesiculitis) or enzyme deficiency.
* Treatment: Oral hydration, mucolytics (N-acetylcysteine), alpha-chymotrypsin enzymatic digestion, and needle syringe shearing in the lab.

### Lecture 4: Assessment of Sperm Morphology & Teratozoospermia Management
<!-- video:id="wRb-WAdKOuQ", duration="18:50", mcNumber=41 -->
* **Video ID:** `wRb-WAdKOuQ` | **Duration:** 18:50 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #41

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Kruger's strict criteria: <4% normal forms indicates severe teratozoospermia.
* Head defects (globozoospermia, acrosomal absence) mandate ICSI with artificial oocyte activation (AOA).
* Lifestyle modification: Cessation of smoking, vaping, hot baths/saunas, and tight synthetic clothing.

### Lecture 5: Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI): Clinical Decision Making
<!-- video:id="SGxf6wmSpdk", duration="23:40", mcNumber=40 -->
* **Video ID:** `SGxf6wmSpdk` | **Duration:** 23:40 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #40

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* DFI >25–30% is associated with reduced blastocyst formation, high miscarriage rates, and repeated IUI/IVF failure.
* Etiologies: Varicocele, leukocytospermia, smoking, obesity, advanced paternal age, and high scrotal temperatures.
* Management: Short ejaculatory abstinence (24 hours), microfluidic sperm sorting (Zymōt), and testicular sperm extraction (TESA/TESE).

### Lecture 6: Role of Antioxidants in Male Fertility: Evidence & Dosages
<!-- video:id="2_JMZTZIakc", duration="17:20", mcNumber=43 -->
* **Video ID:** `2_JMZTZIakc` | **Duration:** 17:20 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #43

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Oxidative stress damages sperm cell membranes and causes single/double-strand DNA breaks.
* Rational regimen: CoQ10 200–300 mg, L-Carnitine 1–2g, Zinc 25–50 mg, Selenium 200 mcg, and Vitamin C/E.
* Avoid excessive antioxidant megadosing which can trigger reductive stress and chromatin decondensation.

### Lecture 7: Micro-TESE for Non-Obstructive Azoospermia (NOA): Practical Pearls
<!-- video:id="HVPEwavswxs", duration="24:10", mcNumber=50 -->
* **Video ID:** `HVPEwavswxs` | **Duration:** 24:10 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #50

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* NOA workup: Serum FSH, LH, Testosterone, Karyotype (47,XXY), and Y-chromosome microdeletions (AZFa, b, c).
* AZFa/b microdeletions: Sperm retrieval is contraindicated (0% success). AZFc microdeletions: ~50–70% retrieval rate via Micro-TESE.
* Operating microscope at 15–25× magnification to selectively identify dilated, opaque seminiferous tubules containing active spermatogenesis.

### Lecture 8: Genetic Evaluation in Male Fertility: Karyotyping & Y-Deletions
<!-- video:id="k0BpKTC4og0", duration="21:30", mcNumber=71 -->
* **Video ID:** `k0BpKTC4og0` | **Duration:** 21:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #71

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Mandatory in severe oligozoospermia (<5 M/mL) and azoospermia.
* Screen for Klinefelter syndrome (47,XXY), Robertsonian/reciprocal translocations, and CFTR mutations in CBAVD.
* Pre-ICSI genetic counseling on transmission of Y-chromosome microdeletions to male offspring.
