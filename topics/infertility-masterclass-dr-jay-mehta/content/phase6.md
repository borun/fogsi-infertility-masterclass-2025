### Lecture 1: Ovum Pick Up (OPU): Surgical Tips and Tricks for High Yield
<!-- video:id="JrXkGNPZzpk", duration="23:45", mcNumber=22 -->
* **Video ID:** `JrXkGNPZzpk` | **Duration:** 23:45 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #22

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Aspiration pressure: 100–120 mmHg with 17G/18G single-lumen aspiration needles.
* Systematic follicle aspiration: Empty all antral follicles from one pole of the ovary to the other without multiple ovarian capsule punctures.
* Flush technique: Reserved for poor responders with few follicles.

### Lecture 2: Technique of Embryo Transfer: Clinical Tips for High Implantation
<!-- video:id="MJ5bYMGm66Q", duration="25:10", mcNumber=26 -->
* **Video ID:** `MJ5bYMGm66Q` | **Duration:** 25:10 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #26

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Full bladder transabdominal ultrasound guidance to straighten the uterocervical angle.
* Soft, flexible catheter (e.g. Cook, Wallace, Guard) with outer sheath remaining in the cervical canal.
* Deposit blastocysts in the upper-to-middle third of the endometrial cavity (1.5–2 cm from the fundus) to prevent fundal irritation and ectopic pregnancy.

### Lecture 3: Fresh vs Frozen Transfer in IVF Cycles: Triage & Evidence
<!-- video:id="wdx7NY6LDyw", duration="24:00", mcNumber=44 -->
* **Video ID:** `wdx7NY6LDyw` | **Duration:** 24:00 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #44

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Indications for Freeze-All: High responders (E2 >3,500 pg/mL, >15 oocytes), Elevated progesterone on trigger day (>1.5 ng/mL), Thin endometrium (<7 mm), Hydrosalpinx, PPOS protocol, and PGT-A.
* Fresh transfer remains valid in normal responders with synchronized trilaminar lining and normal progesterone levels.

### Lecture 4: Embryo Grading (Day 3 vs Day 5) & Time-Lapse Imaging
<!-- video:id="cHbb2fj7Ykg", duration="26:30", mcNumber=33 -->
* **Video ID:** `cHbb2fj7Ykg` | **Duration:** 26:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #33

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Gardner grading system for blastocysts: Expansion (1–6), Inner Cell Mass (A, B, C), Trophectoderm (A, B, C).
* Day 5/6 transfer: Natural selection against aneuploid and arrested embryos.
* Time-lapse imaging: Morphokinetic parameters (t2, t3, t5, cc2, s2) predict euploidy and implantation.

### Lecture 5: Vitrification of Blastocyst Embryos: Live Freezing Technique
<!-- video:id="0-cSp6oF-30", duration="21:20", mcNumber=73 -->
* **Video ID:** `0-cSp6oF-30` | **Duration:** 21:20 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #73

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Ultra-rapid cooling rate (>20,000°C/min) preventing intracellular ice crystal formation.
* Equilibration solution (DMSO + Ethylene Glycol) and Vitrification solution with high sucrose.
* Survival rates exceed 95–98% in experienced embryology laboratories.

### Lecture 6: Poor Quality Embryos & Arrested Embryos: Troubleshooting Guide
<!-- video:id="zEr-tyhkg_Y", duration="22:15", mcNumber=56 -->
* **Video ID:** `zEr-tyhkg_Y` | **Duration:** 22:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #56

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Early cleavage arrest (Day 1–3) usually reflects maternal oocyte cytoplasmic defects or mitochondrial insufficiency.
* Late blastocyst arrest (Day 3–5) indicates embryonic genome activation failure or severe paternal sperm DNA damage.
* Solutions: Calcium ionophore artificial oocyte activation (AOA), microfluidic sperm sorting, and antioxidant therapy.

### Lecture 7: Benchtop vs Box vs Time-Lapse Incubators in the IVF Lab
<!-- video:id="s4fGO3Ncp84", duration="19:50", mcNumber=58 -->
* **Video ID:** `s4fGO3Ncp84` | **Duration:** 19:50 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #58

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Benchtop (mini-chamber) incubators provide ultra-fast temperature and gas recovery (<1–2 minutes) compared to large box incubators.
* Low oxygen culture (5% O2, 6% CO2, 89% N2) reduces reactive oxygen species and improves blastocyst formation.
* Time-lapse systems (EmbryoScope/Geri) eliminate the need to remove dishes from the incubator for morphological assessment.

### Lecture 8: Role of PGT-A & Genetic Embryo Testing in Indian Practice
<!-- video:id="Tl8S1Gr9pJw", duration="24:00", mcNumber=53 -->
* **Video ID:** `Tl8S1Gr9pJw` | **Duration:** 24:00 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #53

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Trophectoderm biopsy on Day 5/6 blastocysts followed by Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS).
* Indications: Advanced maternal age (>37y), recurrent pregnancy loss, recurrent implantation failure, and severe male factor.
* Significantly reduces time-to-pregnancy and miscarriage rates per transfer.
