### Lecture 1: What Is the Normal Endometrial Thickness in Women?
<!-- video:id="xhR6yemY1-E", duration="17:30", mcNumber=29 -->
* **Video ID:** `xhR6yemY1-E` | **Duration:** 17:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #29

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Endometrial dynamics across menstrual cycle phases: Proliferative (4–8 mm), Periovulatory Trilaminar (8–12 mm), Secretory (10–14 mm).
* Cutoff: Minimum acceptable thickness for embryo transfer is ≥7 mm.
* Morphology (Pattern) is equally important as thickness: Grade A (Triple-line) has highest implantation.

### Lecture 2: Understanding & Evaluation of Thin Endometrium in IVF
<!-- video:id="HrvbeT0cUjA", duration="21:40", mcNumber=46 -->
* **Video ID:** `HrvbeT0cUjA` | **Duration:** 21:40 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #46

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Definition: Endometrial thickness <7 mm on day of trigger or progesterone start in FET cycles.
* Etiologies: Prior aggressive curettage (Asherman syndrome), genital tuberculosis, chronic endometritis, or poor basal radial artery blood flow.

### Lecture 5: Chronic Endometritis & Implantation Failure in IVF
<!-- video:id="z-6se-MxTzU", duration="22:50", mcNumber=63 -->
* **Video ID:** `z-6se-MxTzU` | **Duration:** 22:50 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #63

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Diagnostic gold standard: Endometrial biopsy with CD138 (Syndecan-1) immunohistochemistry (≥1–5 plasma cells/HPF).
* Hysteroscopic findings: Micropolyps (<1 mm), hyperemic strawberry pattern, stromal edema.
* First-line treatment: Oral Doxycycline 100 mg BID for 14 days.

### Lecture 6: Role of Probiotics & Endometrial Microbiome in Fertility
<!-- video:id="ApVb1-UUUvU", duration="19:40", mcNumber=66 -->
* **Video ID:** `ApVb1-UUUvU` | **Duration:** 19:40 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #66

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Lactobacillus-dominated microbiome (>90% Lactobacillus crispatus / iners) correlates with high implantation and live birth rates.
* Dysbiosis with pathogenic anaerobes (Gardnerella, Prevotella, Atopobium) causes subclinical inflammation and RIF.
* Treatment: Targeted vaginal probiotic pessaries + oral Lactobacillus reuteri/rhamnosus.

### Lecture 7: Endometrial Preparation Protocols for Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)
<!-- video:id="DeqhOWj1-j0", duration="26:10", mcNumber=24 -->
* **Video ID:** `DeqhOWj1-j0` | **Duration:** 26:10 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #24

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) Cycle: Estradiol valerate 6–8 mg/day with or without GnRH agonist downregulation.
* Natural Cycle / Modified Natural Cycle FET: Utilizing spontaneous ovulation + hCG trigger, optimal for patients prone to preeclampsia.
* Progesterone initiation: Day 5 blastocyst transfer timed exactly on the 6th day of progesterone exposure (120 ± 4 hours).

### Lecture 8: Endometrial Preparation for Embryo Transfer in Adenomyosis
<!-- video:id="dV7WFnlq0SI", duration="25:30", mcNumber=81 -->
* **Video ID:** `dV7WFnlq0SI` | **Duration:** 25:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #81

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Adenomyosis severely disrupts endometrial receptivity and uterine peristalsis.
* Mandatory 1–3 months of GnRH depot downregulation (Leuprolide 3.75 mg or Goserelin 3.6 mg) prior to HRT cycle.
* Co-treatment with Letrozole 2.5 mg during estrogen build-up phase to suppress in-situ aromatase.

### Lecture 12: BLAST Series (Part 4): Low AMH and Adenomyosis Management
<!-- video:id="a37iOZCT9k0", duration="29:15", mcNumber=79 -->
* **Video ID:** `a37iOZCT9k0` | **Duration:** 29:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #79

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* The double challenge: Decreased ovarian reserve combined with hostile endometrial environment.
* Stimulate FIRST to freeze embryos BEFORE giving prolonged GnRH depot downregulation (which can cause irreversible ovarian suppression in low AMH).
* Embryo banking followed by medical or surgical uterine management.
