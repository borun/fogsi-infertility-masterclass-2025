### Lecture 3: Evaluation Protocol for Recurrent Implantation Failure (RIF)
<!-- video:id="sSsjUaMtddw", duration="28:30", mcNumber=65 -->
* **Video ID:** `sSsjUaMtddw` | **Duration:** 28:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #65

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Definition: Failure to achieve pregnancy after transfer of ≥3 euploid blastocysts or ≥4 high-grade cleavage embryos.
* Comprehensive diagnostic checklist: Cavity assessment (3D TVS/Hysteroscopy), CD138 biopsy, thrombophilia panel, thyroid/prolactin, and sperm DFI.

### Lecture 4: Basics of Reproductive Immunology in Failed IVF & Abortions
<!-- video:id="jTlalr83qLk", duration="24:10", mcNumber=61 -->
* **Video ID:** `jTlalr83qLk` | **Duration:** 24:10 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #61

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Maternal-fetal immune tolerance: Role of decidual Natural Killer (dNK) cells, T-regulatory (Treg) cells, and Th1/Th2 cytokine balance.
* Decidual NK cells promote vascular remodeling and angiogenesis, whereas hyperactivated peripheral NK cells can be cytotoxic.

### Lecture 5: Diagnostic Investigations in Reproductive Immunology
<!-- video:id="5DL5YjEQjxw", duration="22:45", mcNumber=62 -->
* **Video ID:** `5DL5YjEQjxw` | **Duration:** 22:45 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #62

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Antiphospholipid antibody (aPL) panel: Lupus Anticoagulant (dRVVT), Anticardiolipin IgG/IgM, and Anti-beta-2-glycoprotein 1 IgG/IgM.
* Antinuclear antibodies (ANA) by indirect immunofluorescence.
* Assessment of peripheral NK cell counts (CD56+ CD16+) and cytotoxicity assays.

### Lecture 6: Role of Low Molecular Weight Heparin (LMWH) & Aspirin
<!-- video:id="n6tsaOtcBb8", duration="21:15", mcNumber=52 -->
* **Video ID:** `n6tsaOtcBb8` | **Duration:** 21:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #52

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Indicated in confirmed Antiphospholipid Syndrome (APS) and hereditary thrombophilias (Factor V Leiden, Prothrombin gene mutation).
* Low-dose Aspirin (75–150 mg/day) + prophylactic Enoxaparin (40 mg/day) initiated from the day of embryo transfer.

### Lecture 7: Role of Intralipids in Recurrent Implantation Failure
<!-- video:id="LSuogLA0sJ0", duration="20:45", mcNumber=64 -->
* **Video ID:** `LSuogLA0sJ0` | **Duration:** 20:45 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #64

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* 20% Intravenous Fat Emulsion (Intralipid): Modulates uterine NK cell cytotoxicity and suppresses excessive proinflammatory Th1 cytokines.
* Infusion protocol: Administered 7–10 days prior to embryo transfer, on the day of transfer, and repeated post-positive pregnancy test.

### Lecture 8: Paternal Lymphocyte Immunization (LIT) Therapy in Recurrent Abortions
<!-- video:id="BrqZivyxxKo", duration="23:15", mcNumber=70 -->
* **Video ID:** `BrqZivyxxKo` | **Duration:** 23:15 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #70

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Immunizing the female partner with purified paternal lymphocytes to induce blocking antibodies.
* Indications: Recurrent pregnancy loss and unexplained RIF with negative maternal autoantibody panels.
* Protocol: Intradermal injections at 4-week intervals prior to conception.

### Lecture 9: Product of Conception (POC) Karyotyping: Practical Points
<!-- video:id="SqITN9I3S3Y", duration="22:50", mcNumber=68 -->
* **Video ID:** `SqITN9I3S3Y` | **Duration:** 22:50 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #68

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Chromosomal aneuploidies account for >60–70% of early first-trimester pregnancy losses.
* Microarray / NGS on POC tissue to differentiate maternal cell contamination and detect copy number variations (CNVs).
