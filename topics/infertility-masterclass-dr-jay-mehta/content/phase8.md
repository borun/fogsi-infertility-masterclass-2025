Laparoscopic cerclage in pregnancy, Shirodkar/McDonald techniques, OHVIRA syndrome, retroperitoneal uterine artery access, and Müllerian anomaly classifications.

* ESHRE/ESGE and ASRM classifications: Septate, Bicornuate, Unicornuate, and Didelphic uteri.
* Hysteroscopic metroplasty for complete septate uterus restores normal cavity geometry and lowers miscarriage rates to baseline.

### Lecture 3: Surgical Management of OHVIRA Syndrome (Müllerian Anomalies)
<!-- video:id="WwAOQ1S5g5w", duration="24:30", mcNumber=98 -->
* **Video ID:** `WwAOQ1S5g5w` | **Duration:** 24:30 | **Series Number:** Masterclass #98

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Obstructed Hemivagina and Ipsilateral Renal Agenesis (OHVIRA / Herlyn-Werner-Wunderlich syndrome).
* Diagnosis: MRI Pelvis + Renal USG.
* Surgical treatment: Complete transvaginal marsupialization and excision of the obstructing vaginal septum.

### Lecture 4: Uterine Artery at Origin: Retroperitoneal Dissection Technique
<!-- video:id="TZfx0FaVx6s", duration="22:10", mcNumber=97 -->

#### High-Yield Clinical Pearls:
* Opening the pararectal and paravesical retroperitoneal spaces to expose the hypogastric (internal iliac) artery bifurcation.
* Identifying the uterine artery anterior branch at its origin to ensure safe clipping in large myomas, AV malformations, and complex pelvic surgeries.
