### Module 2: Tubal Factor Evaluation: ART vs. Surgery
<!-- module:id="d2-m2", speaker="Dr. Damodar Rao", start="00:09:46", startSec=586, endSec=1479 -->
* **Proximal Obstruction:** 10–25% caused by spasm or amorphous debris. *Hysteroscopic Proximal Tubal Cannulation (PTC)* is the procedure of choice (22–30% pregnancy rate, 4% ectopic risk). Resection and anastomosis are discouraged.
* **Sterilization Reversal (Tubo-tubal Anastomosis):** Best in women <30 years with residual tube length ≥ 4–5 cm (65–68% pregnancy rate).
* **Hydrosalpinx:** Salpingectomy or laparoscopic clipping prior to IVF is essential to eliminate toxic fluid reflux and restore implantation rates.
* **Clinical Triage:** Surgery is suited for young women with isolated tubal pathology; IVF is preferred in advanced age, diminished reserve, or severe male factor.

### Module 3: Hysteroscopy: Your Third Eye in Fertility Surgery
<!-- module:id="d2-m3", speaker="Dr. Sanket Pisat", start="00:26:40", startSec=1600, endSec=2455 -->
* **Endometrial Polyps:** Any polyp ≥ 1 cm or present in an infertile patient requires complete excision down to the base using cold scissors or resectoscopy slicing.
* **Uterine Septum:** Diagnosed via 3D TVS using CONUTA/ESHRE criteria (internal indentation >50% of wall thickness). Resection increases cavity volume and improves term delivery.
* **T-Shaped Uterus:** Perform lateral metroplasty only when all three CUME criteria are satisfied; avoid overtreating mild lateral indentations.
* **Submucous Fibroids:** FIGO Types 0–1 via bipolar resectoscope. Transmural FIGO Types 2–5 should be removed laparoscopically to prevent fluid overload and endometrial scarring.
* **Intrauterine Synechiae:** Sharp adhesiolysis followed by a pediatric Foley catheter balloon/stent for 2–4 days and cyclical estrogens.

### Module 4: Evaluating the Ovary in Infertile Couples
<!-- module:id="d2-m4", speaker="Dr. Narendra Malhotra", start="00:42:20", startSec=2540, endSec=4255 -->
* **Quality vs. Quantity:** Female age dictates oocyte euploidy (quality), while AMH and AFC dictate follicle quantity. A younger patient with low AMH has a higher live birth chance than an older patient with normal AMH.
* **Day 2 TVS Protocol:** Count 2–10 mm antral follicles (Normal: 7–14; Low: <5; High/PCOS: >15), measure ovarian volume (3–12 cm³), and evaluate stromal Doppler vascularity.
* **Dynamic Markers:** Follicle-to-Oocyte Index ($\text{FOI} = \frac{\text{Retrieved Oocytes}}{\text{AFC}} \times 100$; normal >50%).
* **Endometriomas:** Perform embryo/oocyte vitrification *before* surgical cystectomy to prevent depleting healthy ovarian cortex.

### Module 6: Chronic Endometritis & Genital Tuberculosis
<!-- module:id="d2-m6", speaker="Dr. Nalini Mahajan", start="01:38:45", startSec=5925, endSec=6825 -->
* **Genital TB:** Silent endosalpingeal and endometrial fibrosis. Requires multi-modal testing (GeneXpert/CBNAAT, AFB culture, hysteroscopy). Treatment requires a complete, uninterrupted **6-month ATT regimen**.
* **Non-Tubercular Chronic Endometritis:** Ascending bacterial infection (*E. coli*, *Enterococcus*, *Mycoplasma*).
* **Diagnosis:** Hysteroscopy (micropolyps, strawberry hyperemia) and endometrial CD138 (Syndecan-1) IHC (diagnostic cutoff: **≥ 5 plasma cells/HPF**).
* **Treatment:** Doxycycline 100 mg BID for 14 days (first-line); Ciprofloxacin + Metronidazole for refractory cases.

### Module 7: Recurrent Implantation Failure: A Clinician's Dilemma
<!-- module:id="d2-m7", speaker="Dr. Sujata Kar", start="01:56:10", startSec=6970, endSec=7864 -->
> [!NOTE]
> **Definition:** Failure to achieve clinical pregnancy after transfer of $\ge 4$ good-quality embryos across $\ge 3$ fresh/frozen cycles in women <40 years.

* Embryonic aneuploidy accounts for ~80% of failures; uterine defects account for ~10%. Transfer of euploid blastocysts reduces true RIF to <5%.
* **Not Recommended:** Routine thrombophilia screening, peripheral NK cell testing, routine ERA, assisted hatching, intralipids, and routine endometrial scratching.
* **Evidence-Supported Actions:** Excision of submucous fibroids/polyps, salpingectomy for hydrosalpinx, and treating chronic endometritis.

### Module 8: Case-Based Panel: Unexplained Infertility Scenarios
<!-- module:id="d2-m8", speaker="Dr. Seema Pandey & Dr. Shrutika (Moderators)", start="02:15:42", startSec=8142, endSec=10715 -->
* **Case 1 (32y, 18m trying, normal basic tests):** Do not prematurely label as unexplained. Perform complete reserve testing (AMH/AFC) and mid-cycle TVS Doppler. Age 32 represents significant decline in Indian women. Plan: Max 3 cycles of Letrozole + Gonadotropins (75 IU) with IUI, then proceed to IVF.
* **Case 2 (29y, 4-5 failed stimulated IUIs, persistent thin lining 6–7 mm):** Indication for combined diagnostic Hystero-Laparoscopy to rule out pelvic endometriosis, peritubal adhesions, and chronic endometritis. Perform pre-menstrual GeneXpert and CD138 IHC. Avoid empirical lateral metroplasty unless strict CUME criteria are met.
