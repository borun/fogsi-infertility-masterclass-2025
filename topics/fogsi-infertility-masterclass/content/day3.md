### Module 2: Patient Selection & Early Referral for ART
<!-- module:id="d3-m2", speaker="Dr. A. J. Mohan", start="00:09:57", startSec=597, endSec=1540 -->
* **Accelerated Aging:** Indian women experience reproductive decline ~6 years earlier than Western counterparts (age 34 in Indian women corresponds to age 40 in Caucasian cohorts).
* **Indications for Direct IVF:** Azoospermia, severe oligospermia, bilateral tubal obstruction, Stage IV endometriosis, and unexplained infertility in women >35 years or duration >2 years.
* **Counseling:** Live birth rates drop below 10% after age 40; maintain realistic expectations.

### Module 3: Role of Hysteroscopy in ART
<!-- module:id="d3-m3", speaker="Dr. Asha Rao", start="00:29:14", startSec=1754, endSec=2763 -->
* Submucous fibroids and endometrial polyps reduce implantation by >50%; hysteroscopic resection normalizes outcomes.
* Meta-analyses demonstrate a statistically significant increase in live birth rates when hysteroscopy is performed after $\ge 1$ failed IVF cycle.
* Resection of endocervical folds/synechiae ("curtain sign") eliminates difficult, traumatic embryo transfers.

### Module 4: Pre-IVF Checklist and Counseling
<!-- module:id="d3-m4", speaker="Dr. Pragya Mishra Choudhary", start="00:51:08", startSec=3068, endSec=4025 -->
* **Medical Optimization:** Strict control of HbA1c, TSH, Prolactin, and Vitamin D. Screening for viral markers and rubella/varicella immunity.
* **Consent Protocols:** Independent, witnessed consent forms for OPU, ICSI, cryopreservation, embryo transfer, and donor cycles.
* **Expectation Management:** Dispel myths regarding 100% success or gender selection; explain the attrition pyramid (follicles → oocytes → blastocysts → euploid embryos).

### Module 5: Adjuvants & Add-Ons in Assisted Reproduction
<!-- module:id="d3-m5", speaker="Dr. Shweta Kauljikar", start="01:09:28", startSec=4168, endSec=4950 -->
* **Supported / Moderate Evidence:** EmbryoGlue (hyaluronan-rich transfer media) improves live birth rates in fresh transfers. DHEA and transdermal testosterone show benefit in low responders.
* **Not Supported for Routine Use:** Routine ERA, intralipids, systemic steroids, routine assisted hatching, routine time-lapse imaging, and intraovarian PRP lack conclusive RCT backing.
* **PGT-A:** Shortens time to pregnancy and reduces miscarriage, but does not increase cumulative live birth rates per started cycle.

### Module 6: Stimulation Protocols in IVF
<!-- module:id="d3-m6", speaker="Dr. Kaberi Banerjee", start="01:29:16", startSec=5356, endSec=6343 -->
* **GnRH Antagonist Protocol:** Current gold standard. Achieves comparable oocyte yield with shorter stimulation and enables an OHSS-free clinic via GnRH agonist triggering.
* **Baseline Criteria:** Day 2 TVS showing thin lining ($\le 5$ mm), absence of functional cysts, and basal $E_2 < 50–70$ pg/mL.
* **Dosing:** Calculated using Age, AMH, AFC, and BMI. Mid-cycle dose escalation ("step-up") causes asynchronous cohorts and should be avoided. Target: 10–15 oocytes.

### Module 7: Day 3 vs. Day 5 Embryo Transfer
<!-- module:id="d3-m7", speaker="Dr. Venugopal M.", start="01:48:42", startSec=6522, endSec=7311 -->
| Parameter | Day 5 Blastocyst Transfer | Day 3 Cleavage Transfer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physiology** | Synchronized with receptive endometrium | Premature uterine exposure |
| **Implantation** | Significantly higher per embryo | Moderate |
| **Embryo Selection** | Natural self-selection of viable embryos | Morphological scoring only |
| **Indication** | Normal/High responders ($\ge 4$ 8-cell embryos) | Poor responders with $< 4$ embryos on Day 3 |

### Module 8: Recent Advances in the Embryology Lab
<!-- module:id="d3-m8", speaker="Dr. Nishad Chimote", start="02:03:36", startSec=7416, endSec=8309 -->
* **AI Grading & Automation:** Platforms such as *Chloe* (lab KPIs), *Violet* (oocyte viability), and *SID* (automated flagellar sperm selection and laser immobilization).
* **Culture Technologies:** Digital microfluidics (EWOD) for automated media renewal under oil; ultra-rapid vitrification protocols (3 min vs. 15 min).
* **Robotic Laboratories:** Enclosed automated platforms (e.g., *Aura*) performing dish preparation, robotic ICSI, and RFID-witnessed cryostorage.

### Module 9: Case-Based Panel: Maximizing ART Success
<!-- module:id="d3-m9", speaker="Dr. Pratik Tambe (Moderator)", start="02:22:48", startSec=8568, endSec=10535 -->
* **Case 1 (36y, Prior Ectopic s/p salpingectomy, Beaded contralateral tube):** High suspicion of Genital TB. Proceed directly to IVF. If transfer fails, perform prophylactic laparoscopic clipping of the diseased tube to prevent recurrent ectopic/hydrosalpinx reflux.
* **Case 2 (34y, BMI 34, Obese PCOS):** Pre-treat with Metformin (up to 1500 mg/day) and lifestyle optimization. Avoid long OCP pre-treatment. Use a Flexible GnRH Antagonist protocol, starting FSH 150–225 IU. Maximum cumulative gonadotropins $\le 3,500$ IU (Cochrane 2023). Mandatory **GnRH Agonist Trigger + Freeze-All strategy** for a 100% OHSS-free cycle.
