### Module 2: How to Interpret Semen Analysis Report (WHO 6th Edition)
<!-- module:id="d1-m2", speaker="Dr. Chaitanya Shembekar", start="00:13:03", startSec=783, endSec=1830 -->
#### WHO 6th Edition Lower Reference Limits
| Parameter | Lower Reference Limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sperm Concentration** | ≥ 16 Million/mL |
| **Total Sperm Number** | ≥ 39 Million/ejaculate |
| **Total Motility (PR + NP)** | ≥ 42% |
| **Progressive Motility (PR)** | ≥ 30% |
| **Strict Morphology (Kruger)** | ≥ 4% normal forms |
| **Vitality (Live Sperm)** | ≥ 54% |

#### Key Clinical Notes:
* **Total Motile Sperm Count (TMSC):** The single best predictive index for reproductive triage:
  $$\text{TMSC} = \frac{\text{Volume} \times \text{Concentration} \times \text{Total Motility}}{100}$$
* **Pre-analytical standard:** 72-hour optimal abstinence window; repeat testing after 4 weeks before making definitive diagnoses.
* **Vitality Test:** Eosin-Nigrosin or Hypo-Osmotic Swelling Test (HOST). Motile sperms are live, but non-motile are not necessarily dead.
* **Absolute contraindications to unassisted fertilization:** Complete globospermia (round-headed sperm lacking acrosome) and complete necrospermia.

### Module 3: How to Approach Azoospermia
<!-- module:id="d1-m3", speaker="Dr. Fessy Louis", start="00:32:49", startSec=1969, endSec=3060 -->
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Azoospermia requires verification by complete pellet examination following high-speed centrifugation (3000g for 15 minutes).

#### Obstructive Azoospermia (OA) vs. Non-Obstructive Azoospermia (NOA)
| Feature | Obstructive Azoospermia (OA) | Non-Obstructive Azoospermia (NOA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Testicular Volume** | Normal (>15–20 mL) | Small, soft (<10–12 mL) |
| **Serum FSH** | Normal (1.5–8.0 IU/L) | Elevated (>8–10 IU/L) |
| **Epididymis / Vas** | Tense / dilated epididymis; possible absent vas | Normal epididymis; vas palpable |
| **Spermatogenesis** | Normal active spermatogenesis | Maturation arrest / Sertoli-cell-only |
| **Sperm Retrieval** | PESA / TESA (~100% recovery) | Micro-TESE (~40–50% recovery) |

### Module 4: Ovulation Induction Protocols for IUI
<!-- module:id="d1-m4", speaker="Dr. Kedar Ganla", start="00:52:52", startSec=3172, endSec=4106 -->
* **Target Goal:** 1–3 mature follicles (18–20 mm) with triple-line endometrium ≥ 7 mm. Avoid high-order multiple gestations.
* **First-Line Regimen:** Letrozole 2.5–5.0 mg/day (Days 2/3 to 6/7). Superior to Clomiphene with higher live birth rates and no anti-estrogenic endometrial thinning.
* **Combined Protocol:** Letrozole (Days 2–6) + HMG/rFSH 75 IU on Day 7 & 9 for improved multi-follicular recruitment.
* **Cancellation Criteria:** Cancel cycle or convert to IVF if $\ge 4$ follicles $\ge 16$ mm or Serum $E_2 > 3,000$ pg/mL to prevent OHSS and high-order twins/triplets.
* **Premature LH Surge Prevention:** Injectable GnRH antagonist (0.25 mg) when lead follicle reaches $\ge 14–16$ mm. Avoid oral antagonists due to teratogenicity.

### Module 5: Semen Preparation Techniques for IUI
<!-- module:id="d1-m5", speaker="Dr. Krishna Chaitanya", start="01:12:21", startSec=4341, endSec=5175 -->
Preparation eliminates prostaglandins (which trigger severe uterine cramps and anaphylactoid shock) and infectious bacteria/WBCs while concentrating motile spermatozoa.

* **Swim-Up Method:** Migration-based technique. WHO standard for normozoospermic samples. Minimal reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation and zero mechanical centrifugation stress.
* **Double Density Gradient (40% / 80% Colloid):** Centrifugation through silica gradient. Optimal for oligozoospermia, viscous, or serodiscordant samples.
* **Benchmark Target:** Post-wash TMSC of **5–10 Million** progressively motile sperm.

### Module 6: Medical Management of Male Infertility
<!-- module:id="d1-m6", speaker="Dr. Kunjimoideen", start="01:29:12", startSec=5352, endSec=6300 -->
* **Hypogonadotropic Hypogonadism:** hCG 1,000–2,000 IU 2–3 times/week + HMG/rFSH 75–150 IU for at least 3–6 months (spermatogenic cycle = 72 days; repeat semen analysis only after 12 weeks).
* **Obesity-Related Hypogonadism:** Testosterone-to-Estradiol (T:E) ratio $< 10$ denotes aromatase hyperactivity. Treat with Letrozole 2.5 mg/day or Anastrozole 1 mg/day for 3–6 months.
* **Anabolic Steroid Abuse:** Cessation of exogenous steroids leads to spontaneous recovery in 4–12 months, supported by hCG therapy.
* **Absolute Contraindication:** Exogenous testosterone therapy in men seeking fertility (suppresses HPG axis and depletes intratesticular testosterone).

### Module 8: Case-Based Panel: Practical Scenarios in IUI
<!-- module:id="d1-m8", speaker="Dr. Deepak Goenka (Moderator)", start="02:12:18", startSec=7938, endSec=10838 -->
* **Donor IUI:** Mild Letrozole stimulation + hCG trigger preferred over natural cycle for accurate timing.
* **Tobacco/Smoking:** Drastically increases sperm DFI and impairs fertilization; mandate cessation before IUI.
* **Lean PCOS:** Prone to severe OHSS. Start with Letrozole 2.5 mg; avoid high starting gonadotropins.
* **Follicular-Endometrial Asynchrony:** Follicle 16 mm with 5 mm lining indicates poor oocyte quality or unresponsive endometrium; cancel and restart rather than adding late estrogens.
* **ART Act Legal Guidelines:** Donor IUI permitted for unmarried women (≥21 with affidavit), divorcees, and widows. Prohibited in abandoned or separated wives without formal legal divorce decrees.
