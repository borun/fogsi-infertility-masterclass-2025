## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Hands-Off Principle: Do not touch the breech until the fetal umbilicus has spontaneously delivered.
* Pinard's maneuver for frank breech; Lovset's maneuver for extended/nuchal arms.
* Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit (MSV) maneuver or Piper forceps for controlled delivery of the aftercoming fetal head.

---

#### Step-by-Step Vaginal Breech Management
* **The 'Hands-Off' Golden Rule:** Never pull on the breech or perform traction prior to delivery of the umbilicus to prevent arm extension over the head and cervical entrapment.
* **Critical Maneuvers:**
  1. **Lovset Maneuver (for extended/nuchal arms):** Grasp fetal pelvis with thumbs on sacrum; rotate fetus 180° so the posterior arm swings anteriorly under the pubic symphysis, delivering the arm by sweeping it across the chest.
  2. **Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit (MSV) Maneuver (for aftercoming head):**
     * Index and middle finger of dominant hand placed over fetal malar bones/maxilla (never in mouth/mandible to avoid jaw fracture).
     * Non-dominant hand supports shoulders and exerts gentle occipital flexion.
     * Controlled flexion and upward arc delivery over the maternal perineum.
  3. **Burns-Marshall Technique:** Grasp feet and swing fetus in wide arc over maternal abdomen.
