## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Principle: Mechanical tamponade of bleeding myometrial sinuses and uterine vascular compression without cavity obliteration.
* B-Lynch suture: Braces the uterine fundus; requires open hysterotomy incision.
* Hayman suture: Modification that does not require opening the uterine cavity; Cho square sutures for localized placental bed bleeding.

---

#### Uterine Compression Suture Masterclass
* **Pre-Suture Compression Test:** Manually compress the uterus with both hands (bimanual uterine compression). If bleeding ceases, compression sutures will succeed.
* **1. Classical B-Lynch Suture:**
  * **Suture Material:** No. 2 Monocryl / Vicryl on 70mm round body needle.
  * **Path:** Enter 3 cm below hysterotomy -> emerge 3 cm above -> loop over fundus 3–4 cm medial to cornua -> enter posterior wall at same level -> emerge posterior -> loop over opposite fundus -> re-enter anterior wall -> tie snug knot while assistant compresses fundus.
* **2. Hayman Suture:**
  * Ideal when hysterotomy is already closed or in PPH after vaginal delivery.
  * Direct transfixation from anterior to posterior wall above lower segment, looping over fundus and tying anteriorly.
* **3. Cho Square Sutures:**
  * Through-and-through square sutures for localized focal placental bed hemorrhage (placenta previa/accreta).
