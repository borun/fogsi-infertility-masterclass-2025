## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Step 1: Unilateral / Bilateral uterine artery ligation (O'Leary suture) at lower uterine segment.
* Step 2: Low uterine artery ligation after bladder reflection for cervical/lower segment bleeding.
* Step 3: Unilateral / Bilateral utero-ovarian anastomotic ligation at ovarian ligament.
* Step 4: Internal iliac (hypogastric) artery ligation; Step 5: Peripartum hysterectomy.

---

#### Stepwise Devascularization Protocol in PPH
> **Systematic Approach to Control PPH & Preserve Fertility**

1. **Step 1: Bilateral Uterine Artery Ligation (O'Leary Technique):**
   * Place No. 1 Vicryl suture 2–3 cm below hysterotomy incision, catching 2–3 cm of myometrium medial to ascending uterine branch to prevent hematoma.
   * Controls ~80% of atonic uterine bleeding.
2. **Step 2: Low Uterine Artery Ligation:**
   * Mobilize the bladder downward by 3–4 cm; ligate descending branch of uterine artery below hysterotomy at cervical junction.
3. **Step 3: Utero-Ovarian Vessel Ligation:**
   * Ligate anastomotic vascular arcade within the broad ligament just medial to the utero-ovarian ligament.
4. **Step 4: Internal Iliac (Hypogastric) Artery Ligation:**
   * Open posterior peritoneum over common iliac bifurcation; trace ureter crossing bifurcation; place right-angled Mixter clamp around anterior division of internal iliac artery 3–4 cm distal to bifurcation and ligate with No. 1 Silk (preserving posterior gluteal branch).
5. **Step 5: Emergency Peripartum Hysterectomy:**
   * Life-saving definitive surgical intervention when devascularization and compression sutures fail to control bleeding.
