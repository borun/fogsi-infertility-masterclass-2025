## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) vs Electric Vacuum Aspiration (EVA): MVA provides constant 60–66 cmHg (24–26 inHg) negative pressure.
* Cervical priming: Sublingual Misoprostol 400 mcg 2–3 hours prior to procedure reduces force required for dilatation.
* Signs of complete evacuation: Gritty uterine cry sensation, red foam in cannula without tissue chunks, and uterine clutching on cannula.

---

#### Surgical Abortion & MVA Protocol
* **Advantages of MVA:** No electricity required, portable, minimal noise (reducing patient anxiety), and lower uterine perforation rates than metal sharp curettage.
* **Cannula Sizing Rule:** Cannula size (in mm) roughly equals gestational age in completed weeks (e.g., 8 mm cannula for 8 weeks gestation).
* **Three Definitive Signs of Empty Cavity:**
  1. **Grit / Cry:** Sensation of crunchy/gritty surface as cannula passes over the bare myometrium.
  2. **Red Foamy Bubble:** Air bubbles and pink foam visible in the transparent syringe/cannula without solid trophoblastic tissue.
  3. **Myometrial Grasping:** Uterine muscle contracts firmly around the cannula, holding it snugly.
* **Post-Procedure Inspection:** Float aspirated tissue in clear water in a kidney dish to identify translucent chorionic villi and gestational sac.
