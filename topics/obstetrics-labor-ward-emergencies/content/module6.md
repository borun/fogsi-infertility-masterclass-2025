## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Deeply engaged head in full dilatation Cesarean: Risk of broad ligament tears and uterine extension.
* Vaginal push technique vs Reverse breech extraction (pull technique): Pulling the feet down from above is significantly safer than pushing from below.
* Use of Patwardhan maneuver in deeply impacted transverse or occipito-posterior presentations.

---

#### Deeply Impacted Fetal Head at Cesarean Section
* **The Problem:** Second-stage Cesarean sections with deeply wedged fetal head in the pelvis frequently cause lower uterine segment extensions into uterine vessels and broad ligament hematomas.
* **Techniques for Extraction:**
  1. **Reverse Breech Extraction ('Pull Technique'):**
     * Reach into fundus, grasp fetal feet, and deliver baby as a breech.
     * **Significantly lower maternal trauma** (lower uterine tears) compared to vaginal push method.
  2. **Vaginal Push Technique:**
     * Assistant places gloved hand vaginally and gently dislodges the vertex upward into the abdominal incision using the cupped palm.
  3. **Patwardhan Maneuver:**
     * For transverse or impacted occipital positions: Extract anterior shoulder, then posterior shoulder, then trunk, with head delivered last.
