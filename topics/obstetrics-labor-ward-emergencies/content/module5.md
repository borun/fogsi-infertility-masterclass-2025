## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Turtle sign: Fetal chin retracts against the perineum immediately after head expulsion.
* HELPERR Sequence: Help, Evaluate for episiotomy, Legs (McRoberts), Pressure (Suprapubic), Enter (Internal maneuvers), Remove posterior arm, Roll (Gaskin).
* Strictly avoid fundal pressure (causes uterine rupture and shoulder impaction) and excessive lateral neck traction (brachial plexus injury).

---

#### The HELPERR Shoulder Dystocia Algorithm
> **Time is Critical:** Complete resolution within 5 minutes to prevent irreversible hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE).

| Step | Action | Execution Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **H** | **Help** | Call for Senior Obstetrician, Anesthesia, Neonatology, and Scrub Team. |
| **E** | **Evaluate / Episiotomy** | Consider generous mediolateral episiotomy to facilitate internal manual maneuvers. |
| **L** | **Legs (McRoberts)** | Hyperflex maternal hips tightly against abdomen. Flattens sacral promontory and increases AP diameter by 1–2 cm (resolves >70% cases). |
| **P** | **Pressure (Suprapubic)** | Apply downward and lateral pressure over fetal anterior shoulder (Rubin I). **NEVER apply fundal pressure!** |
| **E** | **Enter Maneuvers** | **Rubin II:** Push posterior aspect of anterior shoulder towards chest. **Woods Screw:** Rotate posterior shoulder 180°. **Reverse Woods:** Rotate opposite direction. |
| **R** | **Remove Posterior Arm** | Sweep posterior arm across fetal chest; grasp wrist and deliver arm across the perineum. |
| **R** | **Roll Patient (Gaskin)** | Place patient on all-fours (hands and knees). Increases pelvic dimensions.
