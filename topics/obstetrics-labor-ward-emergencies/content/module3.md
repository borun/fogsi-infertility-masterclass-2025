## 🎯 Clinical Learning Objectives
* Prerequisites (FORCEPS mnemonic): Fully dilated, Occlusion of membranes ruptured, Roentgen/position confirmed, Cervix retracted, Empty bladder, Pelvis adequate, Station >= +2.
* Wrigley's vs Simpson's vs Kielland forceps: Low outlet vs low/mid pelvic rotational blades.
* Traction vector: Pajot's maneuver (downward & backward, then curving upward in the axis of the pelvis).

---

#### Operative Forceps Safety Checklist
* **Mandatory Prerequisites Before Application:**
  * **C**ervix fully dilated (10 cm) & retracted.
  * **O**rientation/Position precisely verified by palpating sagittal suture and fontanelles (OA position ideal).
  * **M**embranes ruptured.
  * **P**elvis clinically adequate.
  * **E**mpty bladder (straight catheterization prior to application).
  * **L**evel/Station: Head must be at or below **+2 station** (outlet or low cavity only).
* **Application Technique (Left Blade First):**
  1. Hold left blade in left hand like a pencil; guide along maternal left lateral vaginal wall using right fingers as a protective shoe.
  2. Insert right blade in right hand along maternal right side.
  3. Lock handles easily without force. If blades do not lock effortlessly, unlock and re-check position immediately.
* **Traction Rules:** Traction applied strictly during maternal uterine contractions and pushing efforts along the curve of Carus.
