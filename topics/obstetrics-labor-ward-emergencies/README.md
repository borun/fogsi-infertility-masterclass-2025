# 👶 Obstetric Emergencies & Labor Ward Procedures

Part of the **[MedEd Open Platform](../../README.md)**.

## 📌 Overview

This clinical masterclass series is delivered by guest faculty obstetricians **Dr. Shilpa G B** and **Dr. Padmapriya Vivek** (in collaboration with **Dr. Jay Mehta**, Shree IVF Clinic). 

The course covers essential intrapartum operative skills, labor ward emergencies, and surgical postpartum hemorrhage management:
* **Selective Episiotomy Technique & Three-Layer Repair**
* **Manual Vacuum Aspiration (MVA) for First-Trimester Evacuation**
* **Operative Forceps Delivery & Prerequisites**
* **Vaginal Assisted Breech Delivery (Lovset & MSV Maneuvers)**
* **Shoulder Dystocia Management (HELPERR Algorithm)**
* **Difficult Fetal Head Extraction at Cesarean Section**
* **Uterine Compression Sutures for PPH (B-Lynch, Hayman & Cho)**
* **Stepwise Uterine Devascularization for Intractable PPH**
